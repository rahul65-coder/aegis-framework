# GLOBAL RULES

1. The approved architecture specification is the source of truth.
2. Do not redesign the architecture.
3. Do not silently remove features.
4. Do not simplify required modules.
5. Do not replace approved technologies without approval.
6. Do not invent requirements.
7. Do not skip phases.
8. Do not declare a phase complete without objective evidence.
9. Do not move to the next phase while the current phase is unstable.
10. Evidence is greater than claims.
